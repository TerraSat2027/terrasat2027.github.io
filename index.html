TerraSat

What is TerraSat?

TerraSat is our team for the CanSat competition, a competition by the ESA where students build a can-sized satelite, simulating a real engineering scenario where a team must work together to complete it in limited time.
The primary mission, which is set by the ESA, is to transmit and collect temperature and pressure data.
Our secondary mission is to collect a soil sample, and find what elements are in the soil. The idea is that we would use this on another planet, and see if the soil can sustain life. For this to be possible, the spectroscoper must find; oxygen, hydrogen, carbon, and nitrogen.

Key dates and deadlines

The competition spans across the entire school year, so there are many deadlines we must plan for.

  1. Preliminary Design Review - November 2026
    This is an 8 page document detailing each part of the mission, what has been done, and how. This is very important as it is submited to the ESA.

  2. Critical Design Review - January 2027
    This is the final design review, it is an evaluation on our overall design, progress, technical achievment, scientific value of our CansSat, outreach, and profesional competencies.

  3. Launch Event - February/March 2027
    This is where we finaly get to launch our can. This means we can test the final design, and also collect the data for our primary and secondary mission.

    All of this decides whether or not we make it to the national finals, so the team are working incredibly hard to meet deadlines and make sure everthing works correctly.
