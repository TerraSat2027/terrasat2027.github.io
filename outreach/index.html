Outreach

Why do we do outreach?

Outreach is important as it allows the team to get assistance from outside the school. For example there is the possibility of getting components from a company that is interested in us, or giving us professional assistance for our project. This means we must show ourselves as a professional team who are capable to complete our project.

How are we doing this?

One method of outreach is social media. This is a great way to promote to a younger audience and connect with other students doing CanSat. We have created a TikTok account called "@TerraSatGRP" The aim is to post regular, high-quality videos that will be picked up by the algorithm and hopefully show our videos to those who are interested. The videos will be showing the progress of our team and what people are working on.
Another method of outreach is creating a website. This website is being coded with the languages HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The aim of the website is to provide a centralised area for all outreach projects - any emails or social media videos should have the link to the website, so those interested can read about our project, and even contact us if they wish.
