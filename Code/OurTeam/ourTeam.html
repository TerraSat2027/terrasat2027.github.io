Our Team

Why do we need a team?

Any engineering project requires a team of people, each with different strengths and weaknesses that must be accounted for so the team can efficiently work well together.
Our team is made up of 6 members:

  • Student A
    Project manager; responsible for allocating tasks, and monitoring progress including team finances.

  • Student E
    Outreach; social media manager, and outreach event coordinator.

  • Student H
    Electronics; designing, building, and integrating electrical components.

  • Student L
    Mechanical; in charge of the designing, building, and testing of the mechanical aspects of the project.

  • Student Z
    Software; responsible for designing, writing, and testing the code.

  • Student M
    Software; responsible for designing, writing, and testing the code.

Team strengths:

Mechanical design: student L is incredibly knowledgeble in this topic, including using designing software, and making the components. This allows the team to excell in mechanical design.
Software design: The team has 4 members who are proficient in coding, Students; H, Z, M, and E. This means we can complete many coding projects such as the ground station code, and mission code very quickly.

Team weaknesses:

Electronic design: No one in our team is an expert in electronics, meaning any time we use electrical compenents we must reseach and plan more than usual. This means electrical design has been a difficult part of our project, therefore we have allocated multiple people with the job so we can get everthing completed on time.
